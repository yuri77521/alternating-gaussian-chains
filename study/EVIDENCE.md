## Error budgets in the executable notes

The [Fourier witness](research/exact-clusters/certificate-referee.md) evaluates 16 modes. With $\epsilon=10^{-48}$, each weight error is at most $257\epsilon$, each structure factor lies in $[0,36]$, and its cosine evaluation incurs at most $30\epsilon$. The finite energy error is bounded by $(16/3)(36+257\cdot37+36)\epsilon$. The remaining energy tail is at most $12(16/25)^{289}/[1-(16/25)^{35}]$. Their sum is below $10^{-42}$.

For the quadratic and quartic coefficients, the physical prefactors are $(8/3)c(2\pi)^2$ and $-(14/9)c(2\pi)^4$. With $c<1$, $2\pi<6.3$ and errors in $c,\pi$ below $\epsilon$, each prefactor has magnitude below 2500 and error below $10^4\epsilon$ by the mean value theorem. A bound covering both coefficient errors is
\[
2500\left(16\cdot16^4\cdot257\epsilon+2\cdot17^4(16/25)^{289}\right)
+10^4\epsilon(16\cdot16^4+1)<10^{-34}.
\]
The tail starts at mode 17 and successive majorants have ratio below $1/2$. The error radius for the finite quartic quotient is therefore $(2\cdot10^{-42}+10^{-34}h^2)/h^4$. The note gives the input Taylor and Machin bounds.

For a fixed rounded allocation the site velocities in the floor parameter are affine in the allocation. The sum $\sum_{i<j}(v_j-v_i)^2$ is convex, so its value on the slack simplex is bounded by its maximum over the vertices. At each vertex the velocities, up to a common shift and permutation, are six consecutive integers, giving $\sum_{d=1}^{5}(6-d)d^2=105$. The centered velocities $(0,1,2,0,1,2)$ give 24. With $\|G''\|_\infty<16$, pair coefficient $1/3$ in absolute value, and floor-band length $w$, interpolation of the energy difference costs at most $16(105+24)w^2/24$. Common-shift rounding adds at most $10\rho^2$ for mesh spacing $\rho$. [Full rounding argument](research/six-site/global-probe.md) · [Implementation](code/probe_six_site_transition.py)

## Referee revisions and open boundary

Independent AI referees read the bilingual text and compared the mathematical statements and references against the fixed research corpus. Revisions clarified the per-particle energy normalization, the direction of the cut-gradient transfer and the Bernoulli rounding argument. The width-derivative proof now subtracts the endpoint fractions before multiplying by their common positive factor; the narrower slope enclosures at width 8 are included. The formal-coverage appendix separates the verified identities from their general analytic inequality. The bibliography distinguishes the earlier dilute manuscript's byline from its submitter. Layout revisions moved long kernel/error expressions into displays and kept tables from interrupting proof sentences.

At $h=1/1000$, write $D(s)$ for the minimum on the closed packed-triple face minus the minimum on the full simplex. The lower boundary of the strict-defeat set is $s_*=\inf\{s>9:D(s)>0\}$. Its value and possible coexistence there remain undetermined. The certified $3111$ classification and its numerical root enclosure apply at $(22,1/1000)$. An explicit surrounding phase region remains open.
