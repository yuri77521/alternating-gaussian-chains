## Results of the present study

Under explicit small-floor and width conditions, the analytical result promotes the earlier balanced antipodal trial to an exact classification and gives the general quartic coefficient. For six particles the packed-face optimization applies for $0<q\le17/50$. The global classification over every feasible positive floor applies to the continuous strip $7.99\le s\le9$. The certified widening of the displaced phase applies on $8\le s\le9$. At precisely $(s,h)=(22,1/1000)$ the global geometry has one packed triple and three singletons. At $h=1/1000$, write $D(s)$ for the minimum on the closed packed-triple face minus the minimum on the full simplex. The lower boundary of the strict-defeat set is $s_*=\inf\{s>9:D(s)>0\}$. Its value and possible coexistence there remain undetermined. The global six-site results use analytical reductions and exhaustive interval certificates.
