# v1.0.1 · Exact clusters and global transitions

This release contains the English and Traditional Chinese manuscripts, proof notes, interval certificates, four new Lean declarations, and the scripts and manifests needed to reproduce their outputs. Six numbered results have the same statements and parameter domains as the independently reviewed manuscript.

The revision restores derivations for the Fourier error budgets and rounding bounds, defines the open critical-width question via the infimum of the strict-defeat set, attributes the width-22 trial to its antecedent, and credits the four-site Lean comparison in upstream PR 7. The two PDFs carry the project byline; the Chinese PDF includes a localized phase diagram.
